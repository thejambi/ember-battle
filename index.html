Train your heroes. Then watch the battle unfold.

A hot-seat prototype of the co-op step-battle game. Each battle deals random +, − and • steps to every fighter and resolves them side by side, using the moves your heroes trained. Villains, their moves and the villain deck are set up for you.

Players
Difficulty (clock length)

How a round works

  1. Draw. Each player draws 2 cards (solo: 3).
  2. Prepare. Players take turns spending actions (2 each, solo 3): Train a move onto a hero slot, Teach a Lesson, Ready a Tactic, Call an Ally, Scavenge (draw 1), Recruit a reserve hero, Swap with reserve, or Share a card.
  3. Scout. Each villain's first step is flipped face up from the villain step deck. A + is an ambush, and that lane must fight.
  4. Fight or hold. Holding heals your hero 1 and draws a card, but the clock still moves.
  5. Battle. Battle length comes from the clock. Each step, both sides flip a step card and use the move in that slot. Villains draw from their own step deck, which has more + cards. Damage = attacker's + minus defender's −. Both sides hit at once.
  6. Aftermath. Spoils for defeated villains, replacements for fallen heroes, new villains arrive, and the clock advances.

Steps

  • + Attack, − Defend, • Focus. An empty slot does nothing.
  • Training a move covers the old one. Covered moves still count as training.
  • After each reveal you can react: spend readied Tactics, Courage (redraw your step) or once-per-battle abilities. The preview shows the damage before you resolve.

Winning

  • Defeat every villain, then the Warlord (who fights every lane at once) before the clock runs out.
  • Act II villains bring harder villain moves into the decks.
